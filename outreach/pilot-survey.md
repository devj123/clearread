# ClearRead pilot feedback survey

For the person running the pilot (teacher, tutor, resource room staff): give this to whoever tried ClearRead and Cognitive Mode after they've had it for at least a week, ideally two. A week of real use produces more honest answers than a five-minute first impression does. Paste the questions below into Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, or paper, whichever your students already use. It should take under five minutes to fill out.

Nothing here asks for a name, so keep it that way if you post it publicly, anonymous answers get more honest ones, especially from a teenager filling this out for their own teacher.

### Notes for whoever's running the pilot

Question 8, 9, and 12 are the ones worth reading closely; they're the only three that ask for a specific incident instead of a general impression, and specific incidents are what actually tell you whether something works. A pile of "it was fine" ratings doesn't tell you much. A pile of "I used Return to Task after I fell down a YouTube hole during a worksheet and it actually got me back" does.

If fewer than five people respond, treat the results as anecdotes worth reading individually rather than a pattern worth generalizing from. That's not a knock on the pilot, five students is a real starting point, it's just not a sample size a claim like "80% found it helpful" should be built on.
